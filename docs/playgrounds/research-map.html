research snapshot 2026-08-12

What changed in post-training research?

Foundations supplied the update rules. Open recipes connected data, rewards, and systems. Reasoning RL exposed new scaling behavior and optimizer failures. The 2026 frontier now tests transfer, sharpening, curricula, re-solving, autonomous RL engineering, and richer scientific rewards.

2026 Limits and frontier directions

Cross-domain transfer

Large in-domain RL gains can vanish across reasoning domains.

Easy-to-hard RL

Keep sparse-reward learning inside an informative curriculum.

Power sampling
